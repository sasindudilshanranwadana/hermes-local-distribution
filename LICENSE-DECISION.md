# Distribution license decision

No license is granted for this private-alpha repository yet. It must remain
private while the owner chooses a license for the original installer,
pre-classifier changes, configuration policy, and documentation.

Third-party components retain their own licenses:

- Hermes Agent: MIT
- OmniRoute: MIT
- Mem0: Apache-2.0
- Superpowers: MIT
- Redis and Postgres/pgvector: their upstream licenses

Before a public release:

1. Select and add a repository-level license.
2. Preserve required third-party notices.
3. Confirm the licenses of every additional skill or plugin.
4. Keep items marked `mixed-or-unknown` disabled and out of artifacts.
5. Review trademark and naming implications for a Hermes-derived distribution.
